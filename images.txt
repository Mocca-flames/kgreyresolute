GENERAL STYLE GUIDELINES
========================
- Tone: Professional, serious, trustworthy, modern

- Atmosphere: Day and night shots to show 24/7 capability, clean, crisp, no cartoonish filters
- Composition: Centered, well-framed, cinematic lighting where possible
- File Format: JPEG for photos, PNG if transparency needed
- Optimize for web: Compress without losing quality (target ~200KB max per image)
