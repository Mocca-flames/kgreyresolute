— Who We Are —

A Trusted Security Partner

K Grey Resolute is a fully PSIRA-registered security solutions provider headquartered in Midrand, Gauteng. We deliver comprehensive protection services to businesses, institutions, and high-net-worth individuals across South Africa. Our team comprises former law enforcement and military personnel, bringing real-world experience and tactical expertise to every engagement.

Founded in 2024, we set out to redefine the security industry by combining rigorous training, modern technology, and unwavering reliability. Whether it's armed guarding, CCTV monitoring, or VIP protection, we operate with precision and accountability — because your safety is non-negotiable.
