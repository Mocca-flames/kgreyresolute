— Open Positions —

Current Vacancies

We're hiring skilled security professionals across multiple roles. All positions require PSIRA certification and a valid driver's license unless otherwise stated.

Armed Response Officers

×5 Positions — Midrand / Johannesburg

Patrol and rapid response to alarms and incidents across Midrand and Northern Johannesburg. Must be able to operate armed and maintain professional client interaction.

Requirements:

  • Valid PSIRA license (armed)
  • Firearm competency certificate (handgun & rifle)
  • Valid driver's license (code 8 or higher)
  • Grade 12 / Matric certificate
  • Minimum 2 years armed response experience
  • Ability to work shifts, weekends, and public holidays

Control Room Operators

×3 Positions — Midrand

Monitor CCTV feeds, alarm systems, and GPS-tracked patrols from our 24/7 control room. Coordinate rapid response teams and maintain accurate incident logging.

Requirements:

  • Valid PSIRA license
  • CCTV monitoring certification (advantageous)
  • Computer literacy — MS Office, incident management software
  • Grade 12 / Matric certificate
  • Excellent attention to detail and situational awareness
  • Ability to work overnight shifts (6pm–6am)

CCTV Monitoring Specialists

×2 Positions — Midrand

Advanced surveillance monitoring with AI analytics platform oversight. Identify suspicious behavior, track movements, and support response teams with real-time intelligence.

Requirements:

  • Valid PSIRA license
  • CCTV Level 2 or higher certification
  • Experience with video analytics software
  • Grade 12 / Matric certificate
  • Strong written communication for incident reporting
  • Previous control room or surveillance experience

Security Guards — Various Grades

×10+ Positions — Midrand & Greater Johannesburg

Static guarding and patrol positions available at various client sites — residential estates, corporate parks, retail centers, and construction sites. Multiple grade levels from Grade A to D.

Requirements:

  • Valid PSIRA license (grade appropriate)
  • Grade 12 / Matric certificate
  • Valid driver's license (advantageous)
  • First Aid certificate (advantageous)
  • Good physical fitness and personal presentation
  • Available for immediate start

Tactical Team Members

×4 Positions — Midrand (Deployable)

Join our specialized tactical response unit for high-risk interventions, cash-in-transit support, and VIP close protection backup. Requires advanced tactical qualifications.

Requirements:

  • PSIRA license with tactical endorsement
  • Advanced firearm competency (SOP 3 and 4)
  • Valid driver's license (code 8 or higher)
  • Grade 12 / Matric certificate
  • Tactical security course certification (e.g., SIA, PSRDA)
  • Excellent physical fitness and defensive tactics proficiency
